How FreelanceHub works

Three steps to get a project moving, whichever side you're on.

1

Create your account

Sign up as a client to post work, or as a freelancer to build a profile and showcase your skills.

2

Post or apply

Clients post jobs with a clear budget and deadline. Freelancers browse jobs and send proposals.

3

Work and get paid

Collaborate through the platform, track progress on your dashboard, and complete the project.
